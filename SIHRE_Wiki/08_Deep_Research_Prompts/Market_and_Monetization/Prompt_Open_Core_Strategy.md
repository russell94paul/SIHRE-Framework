# Deep Research Prompt — SIHRE Open-Core Strategy

## Prompt

```text
Create a strategic report on whether SIHRE should be open, closed, or open-core.

Important boundaries:
- Treat SIHRE as a public, domain-general framework.
- Do not include trading, finance, alpha generation, execution logic, or sensitive domain examples.
- Do not disclose implementation-specific system names, exact modality counts, weights, thresholds, schemas, or private benchmarks.
- Use neutral examples from robotics, industrial monitoring, cyber defense, logistics, scientific discovery, healthcare decision support, climate systems, or manufacturing.


Evaluate:
- public whitepaper only
- open reference architecture
- open SDK
- open benchmark suite
- closed enterprise control plane
- paid certification
- paid domain adapters
- paid governance dashboard
- risks of copycats
- adoption benefits of openness

Final output:
- recommendation
- public/private boundary
- license strategy
- community-building strategy
- revenue impact
- risk mitigations
```
