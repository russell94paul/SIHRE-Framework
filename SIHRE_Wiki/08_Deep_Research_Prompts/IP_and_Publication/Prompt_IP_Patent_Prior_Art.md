# Deep Research Prompt — SIHRE IP and Patent Prior Art

## Prompt

```text
Create a deep research report on SIHRE intellectual property strategy.

Important boundaries:
- Treat SIHRE as a public, domain-general framework.
- Do not include trading, finance, alpha generation, execution logic, or sensitive domain examples.
- Do not disclose implementation-specific system names, exact modality counts, weights, thresholds, schemas, or private benchmarks.
- Use neutral examples from robotics, industrial monitoring, cyber defense, logistics, scientific discovery, healthcare decision support, climate systems, or manufacturing.


Research:
- patentability of architecture-level AI systems
- prior art in compound AI systems
- prior art in meta-orchestration
- prior art in mixture-of-experts and expert routing
- prior art in AI governance/control planes
- prior art in adaptive trust scoring
- trade secret vs patent trade-offs
- defensive publication strategy
- trademark strategy for SIHRE
- open-core IP implications

Final output:
- IP risk map
- possible patent claim families
- what should remain trade secret
- what can be safely published
- trademark checklist
- patent attorney preparation notes

Note:
This report is not legal advice and should be used to prepare for professional counsel.
```
