# Deep Research Prompt Index

Use these prompts to generate additional SIHRE research docs. Recommended order:

1. Competitive landscape
2. Meta-orchestrator deep dive
3. Evaluation benchmarks
4. IP and patent prior art
5. AI governance / compliance
6. Monetization market validation
7. Control-plane product
8. Certification standard
9. Open-core strategy
10. Academic venue strategy

Each prompt is written to avoid domain-sensitive disclosure and keep SIHRE domain-general.
