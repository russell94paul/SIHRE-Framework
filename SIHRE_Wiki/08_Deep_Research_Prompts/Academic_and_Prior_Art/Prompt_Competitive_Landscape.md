# Deep Research Prompt — SIHRE Competitive Landscape

## Prompt

```text
Create a deep research report on the competitive landscape around SIHRE — Self-Improving Heterogeneous Reasoning Ensemble.

Important boundaries:
- Treat SIHRE as a public, domain-general framework.
- Do not include trading, finance, alpha generation, execution logic, or sensitive domain examples.
- Do not disclose implementation-specific system names, exact modality counts, weights, thresholds, schemas, or private benchmarks.
- Use neutral examples from robotics, industrial monitoring, cyber defense, logistics, scientific discovery, healthcare decision support, climate systems, or manufacturing.


Research categories:
1. Compound AI systems
2. Agent orchestration platforms
3. LLMOps and MLOps platforms
4. AI governance platforms
5. Model monitoring and observability
6. Decision intelligence systems
7. Multi-agent frameworks
8. Mixture-of-experts and expert-routing systems
9. Adaptive/continual learning systems
10. Autonomous scientific discovery systems

For each category:
- define the category
- list representative companies/projects/papers
- explain how SIHRE is similar
- explain how SIHRE differs
- identify white-space opportunities
- identify threats and copycat risks

Final output:
- executive summary
- competitor map
- positioning matrix
- defensibility analysis
- recommended first wedge
- messaging recommendations
- research citations
```
