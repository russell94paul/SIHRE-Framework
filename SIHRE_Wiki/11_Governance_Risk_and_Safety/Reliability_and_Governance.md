# SIHRE Reliability and Governance

## Governance principles

- Log every routed reasoning path.
- Track evidence provenance.
- Track expert disagreement.
- Track uncertainty and calibration.
- Track verification outcomes.
- Track simulation escalation.
- Track abstentions and deferrals.
- Track expert reliability over time.
- Use human oversight in high-stakes contexts.
- Avoid unrestricted self-modification.

## Reliability risks

- orchestrator overfitting
- synthetic scenario bias
- correlated expert failure
- unreliable critics
- memory contamination
- overconfident synthesis
- drift blindness
- expert monoculture

## Mitigations

- ablations
- audit logs
- calibration checks
- red-team verification
- drift tests
- human review
- controlled updates
- conservative abstention
