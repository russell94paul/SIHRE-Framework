# SIHRE Monetization Model, Profit Scenarios, and Go-to-Market Plan

**Framework:** SIHRE — Self-Improving Heterogeneous Reasoning Ensemble  
**Purpose:** Internal business planning page for monetizing SIHRE as a standalone asset outside NeuroSpect Tier 3.  
**Status:** Draft planning model. Numbers are illustrative assumptions, not guarantees.

---

## 1. Core Monetization Thesis

SIHRE should not be sold as “a model.” It should be positioned as a **reasoning-governance framework** and eventually as a **control plane for heterogeneous AI systems**.

The highest-value framing:

> SIHRE is a framework for coordinating multiple reasoning modalities through a meta-orchestrator that manages trust, uncertainty, verification, memory, and adaptation over time.

This makes SIHRE useful beyond one app or one domain.

Modern AI systems are becoming complex collections of models, agents, tools, validators, retrieval systems, and memory layers. Most teams lack a reliable way to govern routing, uncertainty, disagreement, drift, and escalation.

Agentic AI adoption is accelerating, but many deployments struggle with cost, reliability, unclear ROI, and auditability.
