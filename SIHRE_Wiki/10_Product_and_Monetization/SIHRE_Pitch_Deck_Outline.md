# SIHRE Pitch Deck Draft Outline

## Slide 1 — Title

**SIHRE**  
Meta-Orchestrated Heterogeneous Reasoning for Adaptive Intelligence Under Non-Stationarity

## Slide 2 — Problem

AI systems are becoming complex combinations of models, tools, agents, retrieval layers, validators, memories, and domain systems.

Most teams do not have a clear way to answer:

- Which expert should be trusted?
- When should the system escalate?
- How should disagreement be handled?
- When should the system abstain?
- How should reliability be updated after outcomes?

## Slide 3 — Why Now

Agentic AI and compound AI systems are growing quickly, but many deployments struggle with unclear ROI, reliability, governance, auditability, and drift.

## Slide 4 — SIHRE Solution

SIHRE is a meta-orchestrated framework for governing heterogeneous reasoning systems under uncertainty, drift, and non-stationarity.

## Slide 5 — Architecture

Show:
- evidence ingestion
- persistent memory
- heterogeneous reasoning modalities
- verification
- simulation
- meta-orchestrator
- feedback loop

## Slide 6 — Initial Offer

**SIHRE Architecture Audit**

A structured assessment of an AI system’s reasoning architecture, expert routing, uncertainty handling, drift readiness, and governance gaps.

## Slide 7 — Long-Term Product

**SIHRE Control Plane**

A dashboard/API for:
- expert registry
- routing logs
- uncertainty tracking
- disagreement detection
- drift monitoring
- verification records
- simulation triggers
- reliability history
- audit traces
