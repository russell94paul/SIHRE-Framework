# SIHRE Control Plane — Draft PRD

## Product concept

The SIHRE Control Plane is software for governing heterogeneous AI systems.

## Target users

- AI engineering teams
- enterprise AI governance teams
- agentic AI builders
- model reliability teams
- industrial AI teams
- research automation teams

## MVP features

1. Expert registry
2. Task routing log
3. Expert output capture
4. Uncertainty summary
5. Disagreement detection
6. Drift indicator integration
7. Verification escalation log
8. Simulation escalation log
9. Expert reliability history
10. Audit-ready decision trace

## User story

As an AI platform owner, I want to understand which experts were invoked, why they were trusted, where they disagreed, and how outcomes updated future routing.

## Non-goals

- Replacing all model monitoring tools
- Training foundation models
- Guaranteeing correctness
- Releasing proprietary domain logic
