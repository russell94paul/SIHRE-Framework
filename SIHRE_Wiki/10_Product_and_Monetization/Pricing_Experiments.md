# SIHRE Pricing Experiments

## Early pricing experiments

| Offer | Test price | Notes |
|---|---:|---|
| SIHRE Architecture Audit | $2,500–$10,000 | Good early consulting offer |
| Meta-Orchestrator Blueprint | $5,000–$20,000 | Higher-value technical strategy |
| Governance Readiness Assessment | $1,500–$7,500 | Enterprise-friendly |
| Research report bundle | $99–$999 | Lower-ticket authority product |
| Workshop | $1,000–$10,000 | Depends on buyer |
| Control Plane MVP | pilot pricing | Validate before scaling |

## Pricing principle

Charge for decision value, not document length.
