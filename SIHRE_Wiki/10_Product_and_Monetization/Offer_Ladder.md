# SIHRE Offer Ladder

| Level | Offer | Price model | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | Whitepaper + website | Free | Build credibility |
| Low | Paid report bundle | Fixed price | Educate buyers |
| Mid | Architecture audit | Fixed project | First serious monetization |
| Mid | Meta-orchestrator blueprint | Fixed project | High-value design work |
| High | Enterprise implementation advisory | Retainer | Strategic revenue |
| Product | SIHRE Control Plane | SaaS / enterprise | Scalable product |
| Ecosystem | Certification | Annual fee | Brand and standards |
| Ecosystem | Partner license | Annual / rev share | Distribution |

## Recommended first paid offer

**SIHRE Architecture Audit**

Deliverables:
- architecture map
- reasoning-modality inventory
- orchestration gap analysis
- reliability risk map
- evaluation protocol
- improvement roadmap
