# Deep Research Prompt — SIHRE Domain Adaptation

## Prompt

```text
Create a research report on domain adaptation in SIHRE.

Important boundaries:
- Treat SIHRE as a public, domain-general framework.
- Do not include trading, finance, alpha generation, execution logic, or sensitive domain examples.
- Do not disclose implementation-specific system names, exact modality counts, weights, thresholds, schemas, or private benchmarks.
- Use neutral examples from robotics, industrial monitoring, cyber defense, logistics, scientific discovery, healthcare decision support, climate systems, or manufacturing.


Core question:
How can a domain-general SIHRE framework adapt to different operating domains without hardcoding the architecture to one domain?

Cover:
- domain ontologies
- evidence interfaces
- memory schemas at a public level
- domain-conditioned routing
- validation metrics
- causal transfer
- invariant representations
- synthetic environments
- human oversight
- risk-specific governance

Final output:
- domain-general vs domain-specific table
- public-safe examples
- recommended domain adaptation playbook
- evaluation framework
- open research questions
```
