# Deep Research Prompt — SIHRE Academic Venue Strategy

## Prompt

```text
Create a research report on where and how to publish SIHRE academically.

Important boundaries:
- Treat SIHRE as a public, domain-general framework.
- Do not include trading, finance, alpha generation, execution logic, or sensitive domain examples.
- Do not disclose implementation-specific system names, exact modality counts, weights, thresholds, schemas, or private benchmarks.
- Use neutral examples from robotics, industrial monitoring, cyber defense, logistics, scientific discovery, healthcare decision support, climate systems, or manufacturing.


Research:
- arXiv categories
- workshops on agentic AI
- workshops on AI governance
- workshops on adaptive systems
- conferences relevant to compound AI systems
- position paper venues
- technical report repositories
- DOI and citation practices
- formatting expectations

Final output:
- recommended publication route
- target venues
- submission requirements
- publication timeline
- paper revision checklist
- risks
```
