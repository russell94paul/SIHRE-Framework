## Introducing SIHRE

Today I am introducing **SIHRE — Self-Improving Heterogeneous Reasoning Ensemble**.

SIHRE is a public research framework for adaptive reasoning systems that need to operate under uncertainty, drift, and non-stationarity.

The central idea is simple: as AI systems become more complex, the important question is no longer only “which model is best?” It is also:

> Which reasoning pathway should be trusted in this context, and how should the system know when to challenge, escalate, defer, or improve?

SIHRE proposes a meta-orchestrated architecture for answering that question.
