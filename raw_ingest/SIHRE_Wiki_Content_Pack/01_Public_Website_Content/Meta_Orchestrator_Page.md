# SIHRE Meta-Orchestrator Page

## Definition

The SIHRE meta-orchestrator is a reasoning-governance layer that selects, sequences, weights, challenges, suppresses, or promotes heterogeneous reasoning modalities based on task context, uncertainty, disagreement, evidence quality, historical reliability, and observed outcomes.

## Responsibilities

- Task interpretation
- Expert routing
- Trust weighting
- Uncertainty assessment
- Disagreement detection
- Verification escalation
- Simulation escalation
- Abstention or deferral
- Memory update
- Expert reliability update
- Self-pruning recommendation
- Expert expansion recommendation

## Why it matters

Without orchestration, heterogeneous systems can become fragile collections of tools. The meta-orchestrator turns SIHRE into an adaptive reasoning architecture.
