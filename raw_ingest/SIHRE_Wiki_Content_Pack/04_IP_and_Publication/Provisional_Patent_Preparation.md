# SIHRE Provisional Patent Preparation Notes

## Purpose

Prepare materials for an IP attorney before public disclosure.

## Bring to attorney

- Whitepaper draft
- Architecture diagrams
- Meta-orchestrator report
- Domain adaptation report
- Public/private boundary table
- Invention timeline
- Prior art notes
- Implementation notes kept separate
- Specific mechanisms that may be novel

## Questions for counsel

1. What is patentable vs abstract?
2. What should be filed before publication?
3. What should remain trade secret?
4. Does the whitepaper disclose too much?
5. Should claims focus on orchestration, governance, or evaluation?
6. What jurisdictions matter?
7. What are public disclosure risks?

## Reminder

Do not publish sensitive mechanisms before legal review if patentability matters.
