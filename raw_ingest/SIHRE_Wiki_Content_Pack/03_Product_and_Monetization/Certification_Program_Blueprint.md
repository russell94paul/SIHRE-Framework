# SIHRE Certification Program Blueprint

## Certification concept

A future SIHRE certification program would assess whether an AI system follows SIHRE-style reasoning governance principles.

## Possible levels

- SIHRE-Aware
- SIHRE-Compatible
- SIHRE-Orchestrated
- SIHRE-Governed
- SIHRE-Certified Adaptive System

## Assessment areas

- Heterogeneous reasoning
- Meta-orchestration
- Evidence provenance
- Expert reliability tracking
- Uncertainty handling
- Drift detection
- Verification escalation
- Simulation/stress testing
- Abstention and deferral
- Auditability

## Launch condition

Do not launch certification until SIHRE has public recognition and at least a small ecosystem of interested users.
