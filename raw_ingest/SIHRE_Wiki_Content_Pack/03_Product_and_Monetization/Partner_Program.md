# SIHRE Partner Program

## Concept

The SIHRE Partner Program would allow consultants, integrators, and AI platforms to build SIHRE-compatible systems.

## Partner types

- Implementation partners
- Research partners
- Tooling partners
- Domain adaptation partners
- Certification partners

## Partner benefits

- Use of SIHRE-compatible language
- Access to reference architecture
- Training materials
- Evaluation checklists
- Certification pathway

## Risks

- Brand dilution
- Low-quality implementations
- Premature standardization
- IP leakage
